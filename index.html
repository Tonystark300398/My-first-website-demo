    How it works

    1. Describe the shot

      Say who or what is in it, what happens, where, and how the camera moves.

    2. An AI model films it

      Your prompt goes to LTX-Video, an open model running on a free Hugging Face GPU. If others are using it, you wait in a short queue.

    3. Watch and download

      The MP4 plays right here. Download it before you close the tab.

    Prompt tips

    The model only knows what you tell it. Short, vague prompts give vague clips.

    • Subject and action. "A cyclist rides" beats "a bike".
    • Setting and light. Time of day, weather, indoor or outdoor.
    • Camera. Close-up, wide shot, slow pan, drone shot, tracking shot.
    • English works best. Other languages work, but less reliably.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The default engine runs on Hugging Face's free GPUs. Each visitor gets a limited amount of free GPU time; when it runs out, wait a while or add a free Hugging Face token in Settings for more.

    Why does it sometimes wait in a queue?

    The free server is shared with everyone using it. The viewfinder shows your place in line.

    How long can videos be?

    2 to 5 seconds on the free engine. Google Veo (paid) makes 4 to 8 second clips with sound, up to 4K.

    Can I use Google Veo instead?

    Yes. Open Settings, pick a Veo engine and paste a Gemini API key from a Google Cloud project with billing turned on. You pay Google per second of video.

    Where do my prompts and keys go?

    Prompts go to the engine you choose (Hugging Face or Google). Keys are saved only in your own browser and are sent only to that engine.
